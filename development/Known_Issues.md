# Known Issues and Open Questions

Updated 2026-11-01.

## Content and design gaps

1. **Thin replay loop.** Best scores and fastest victory times are stored per operation and difficulty. Since 2026-11-01 results show the round's workout (punches, crits, chain, active time, a calorie estimate) and call a new best time and a new best score, and the cards and the Breach Night map show the best score with a bronze, silver, or gold medal ([Workout_Stats.md](Workout_Stats.md)). There is still no endless mode or leaderboard. A full campaign run is about 40 minutes with little reason to return. Phase 6 in the roadmap.
2. **No narrative hook.** Six operations with debrief lines, but nothing larger at stake and no payoff for the Overseer thread. The Maw's boss fight is reached at Full Resonance; at other difficulties The Maw differs from other rifts only in size and spawn rate, with the occasional tentacle glimpse. Phase 7.
3. **World map is local and unplaytested.** Built 2026-10-26 (`development/World_Map_Design.md`). The fronts are authored, not moved by a server; the seeded operation counts give a fresh profile a war that is already partly fought. Districts hand their site id to `ArenaPresentation.apply_site` before the deploy (`BreachSites.DISTRICTS` maps all sixteen to the seven sites; validated), but no site has been seen from a world mission on the headset yet. Since 2026-11-01 the world map is the boot screen and Breach Night is gone from the menus; the fronts recede as cities are liberated but never advance, by decision, until the war has a heartbeat. Needs a headset pass against the world map sections of `Menu_UX_Test_Plan.md`, including a receded front and a liberated city.
4. **One arena.** Every operation is the same dead-city breach site. The "RSF // ERM TRAINING CHAMBER 07" sign (`Scripts/Presentation/arena_builder.gd`) now shows only while training runs, under the chamber dome. Phase 8.
5. **OP-04 and OP-05 share one wave table.** Only rift placement differs (`_paired_assault_waves` in `Scripts/Core/mission_catalog.gd`).
6. **Every phantom aims at head height.** Targets land 18 to 30 cm below the eyes. No low approaches, so below Full Resonance squats and uppercuts come only from the Angler's lure position; at Full Resonance The Maw's boss asks for them.
7. **Expected mission times are estimates until the run log has real ones.** Every card's `expected_minutes` (catalog and districts) is `WorldMap.expected_minutes` of the waves: a hard puncher's clean run (1.3x rift damage) to an average one with half again for misses. The old low ends were the average clean run, and on 2026-10-24 the developer finished operations under them; the 2026-11-01 retune moved the low ends to cover punch power (First Light 2 to 3, Widen the Ring 5 to 9, Chen's Gambit 5 to 8, The Maw 3 to 6; Double Breach and Open Arc 6 to 10). Validation fails if any tier's estimate leaves its card (`Tests/difficulty_audit.gd`, report in `reports/difficulty_audit.md`). Still not timed: every round now writes a line to `user://run_log.csv` on the headset, and `tools/pull_run_log.ps1` compares actual with expected. On paper The Maw (4.2 min at Standard) and all four district kinds (3 to 3.5 min) run under the roadmap's five-minute floor; real runs decide whether to lengthen them. See [Difficulty_Tuning.md](Difficulty_Tuning.md).
8. **Difficulty numbers are untested on a headset.** `Scripts/Core/difficulty_tier.gd`: Assist is 0.85 speed, 1.25 tells, 0.96 rift health, 1.04 spawn interval, 0.6 contact; Operator 1.12, 0.85, 1.04, 0.96, 1.25; Full Resonance 1.0 speed, 1.0 tells, 0.5 interval, 1.0 contact, two more live phantoms, and health 1.1x then scaled per rift group by how much faster the feed lets the player kill (`DifficultyTier._scale_health_with_pace`), since 2026-11-01: the developer found Full Resonance too easy on the headset and asked for a phantom every one to two seconds, and health rose with the feed so the length holds. The first guesses (Assist 0.8 health and 1.15 interval, Operator 1.15 and 0.9) moved paired missions and The Maw by 12 to 20 percent, because the player's kill cadence, not the feed, sets the pace there; retuned 2026-11-01 so Assist and Operator run within 5 percent of Standard everywhere and Full Resonance 8 to 10 percent longer, a hold's team time scaling with the tier's health. Cards show the same range at every tier except The Maw at Full Resonance (4 to 7 minutes). Whether the tiers feel right in challenge (speed, tells, contact) still needs testers; whether they keep their length needs the run log at each tier.
9. **The sweep's bend check runs at every difficulty.** Below Full Resonance a sweep is duck-depth only (`squat_depth` capped at 0.12), where the exploration doc says a bend should clear with the bonus; `ResonanceSweep` still withholds the bonus for a bend whenever the squat detector is calibrated. Only the debug Sweep Drill has sweeps below Full Resonance today, so nothing in the campaign hits it. Decide before Standard maps get duck sweeps (decision 2).
10. **Full Resonance outside the boss.** Since 2026-11-01 the six operations are debug-only and the tier comes from the city's state, so Full Resonance is what every overrun city plays at: a tenth more rift health, and the boss at a Shut the Maw district. Outside The Maw it asks nothing extra of the body. Squat-depth sweeps in every rift were tried and removed the same day (2026-10-13): nothing in the fiction swings a blade from an ordinary rift, and "bosses carry the movements" stands. If Full Resonance needs movement outside bosses before the war map, the candidate with a source is a short tentacle lash from the rift (the boss tentacle driving a sweep), which would foreshadow the bosses. Push-ups count from head height; the controllers can hang with the hands tracked (see Technical follow-up). A Full Resonance Maw that holds seals without the glimpse.
11. **Training has had one headset session.** On 2026-10-17 the developer played it through once and was happy with it apart from timing: Chen's instructions came after the recruit could act. They now play while the phantom flies in (Playtest_Log.md); not yet re-checked. Still to feel on Quest: whether 0.95 m is the right distance for a paused Angler's lure to sit inside a jab, the glove check, where the floating cards and tags sit against the paused phantom, whether the punch power band (2 to 6 m/s) matches real controller speeds so a tap and a hard hit really land at each end, and how training reads at Full Resonance (it always runs at Standard). See [Tutorial_Plan.md](Tutorial_Plan.md).
12. **The Maw boss played the same attack order every fight.** Reported 2026-10-24 after several Full Resonance runs. Fixed the same day: the first cycle is still the authored `CYCLE` in `Scripts/Bosses/maw_boss.gd`, every cycle after it shuffles the four blocks (two high sweeps, slam with summon, volley) with the summon before the volley, the jump pair closing, and no repeat of the previous order. Tells, speeds, and rests are unchanged. Not yet felt on the headset.
13. **A harder punch scores less over a mission.** Each phantom scores the same however hard it is hit, but a harder punch, and a lure crit with its double rift damage, closes a rift in fewer phantoms. Over a whole mission a soft, crit-less player who keeps the chain can out-score a hard hitter, which runs against a fitness game. Medals inherit it: par assumes a firm punch (1.15x), and a clean no-crit run at a soft punch reaches gold on most missions. Not changed on 2026-11-01 (no phantom or scoring changes on that pass). Candidates: score by rift damage dealt, or give crits points without the extra rift damage. See [Workout_Stats.md](Workout_Stats.md).
14. **Medal thresholds are a model.** Silver at 70% and gold at 100% of a simulated par run per mission and tier (`MissionMedals`). First Light's first rift feeds every 4.2 s, past the 4 s combo timeout, so its chain holds only when kills bunch up. Retune from testers' scores.

## Audio

- Chen's clips are generated; listen through once in headset and run `tools/check_vo.ps1` after any script change. Any clip the check flags gets regenerated with `-Only <event>`.
- Training's 59 `tutorial_` events (70 clips) and the 12 lines that changed "operator" to "Striker" were voiced on 2026-10-13 and pass `check_vo.ps1`. Not yet heard in headset; listen to the introduction especially, the one long speech.
- Missions have no time limit and no timeout outcome, so Chen has no clock lines. If completion time later feeds medals or a race mode, decide whether she should call a pace.
- Impact, UI, and proximity audio have not had a final mix on headset speakers.

## Comfort and feel to validate

- **Strike window.** A punch only counts once the phantom is within `strike_reach` (1.7 m) or in its commit phase; earlier hits buzz and are rejected (`Scripts/Phantoms/phantom.gd`). The developer has not felt this as a problem in headset. Validate with testers who have not been told the rule before deciding whether early hits should become a reduced-score deflect.
- **Damage tint.** The possession tint and distortion fill the whole view at full saturation in desktop captures. Confirm the strength in headset and make sure Reduced Flashes caps it.

## Technical follow-up

- Results pointer selection after defeat was unreliable on Quest 3. Fixes landed on 2026-10-03 and 2026-10-04 (pointer reset on results entry, trigger-release selection). Retest victory and defeat on headset and close this item.
- The recorded release APK predates the October content. Rebuild and re-sign from current `main` before distribution. Back up the release keystore and password off-machine first.
- Android export automation can be added to CI only after the export preset and build template versions are established on a trusted workstation.
- The validation runner now covers stragglers and the own-music view; round and rift integration fixtures for whole operations are still missing.
- `_validate_arc_motion` in `Tests/validation_runner.gd` is flaky: "Yellow arc stalled during approach" failed 2 of 5 headless runs on 2026-10-09 with no code change between runs. The arc is built from random side, bow, and control-point values, and 120 physics steps is not always enough for every curve to clear 1 m at 0.3 m/s. Seed the RNG in the test or lengthen the run.
- **Every punched phantom flew to the player's left.** Reported 2026-10-26 from the headset; fixed the same day. Since ab4bdee (2026-10-24) the Meta recenter calls `XRServer.center_on_hmd`, which turns the controllers' transforms by the new reference frame but not `XRPose.linear_velocity`, so the strike direction in `Scripts/Player/hand_collision.gd` stayed in the old play-space frame and every fling came out turned by the recenter's yaw. The velocity now takes the reference frame, the origin's turn, and the world scale; `_validate_punch_direction` covers both hands. Not yet felt on the headset.
- The project targets Compatibility rendering for standalone Quest reliability. Reassess Mobile/Vulkan only with device measurements on the pinned Godot release.
- **Hand tracking: simultaneous hands and controllers built.** Tests 7 and 8 (2026-10-14 and 2026-10-17, [Playtest_Log.md](Playtest_Log.md)) ruled out tracked hands for the fighting: 4 to 10 dropped punches in 20 against a bar of 2, lost hands at the face and on wide hooks, visible lag. Simultaneous hands and controllers works: controllers hanging on their straps, hands tracked for push-ups with no drops (decision 39). Built 2026-10-17 ([Exercise_Mechanics_Exploration.md](Exercise_Mechanics_Exploration.md), boss section, "Hand tracking"): a free hand cannot strike, its glove and gauntlet ride the tracked hand, fist and pinch work. On the floor the runtime often takes a hanging controller that touches the hand for a held one, so the gauntlets stay with the controllers; a plank needs both hands down either way (a flat palm, or a hanging controller within 30 cm of the floor), and a boss fight the same day counted 10 of 10 push-ups twice. Chen's spent-tentacle lines now say push-ups plainly.
