# Difficulty audit

Generated by `Tests/validation_runner.gd` from the wave tables (`Tests/difficulty_audit.gd`). Do not edit by hand; run validation to regenerate. Nothing here is a timed run: `user://run_log.csv` holds those (see `development/Difficulty_Tuning.md`).

How to read it:

- **Phantoms** are the kills a clean run takes at 13 rift damage per kill (`WorldMap.KILL_DAMAGE`); the range is a hard puncher (1.30x) to a soft one (0.85x). A hold's team takes the rest of its rift; a boss row adds the anchor's work.
- **Peak live** is the most phantoms the open rifts may have out at once. **Spawns/min** is the open rifts' feed, weighted by how long each is open.
- **Tell s** is the mean tell over each wave's pool (species base tell times `telegraph_scale`, never under its floor). **Speed** is the mean `speed_scale`.
- **Est. min** is `WorldMap.run_seconds`: one kill per spawn interval but no faster than one per 2 s, 6 s per rift opening, pairs capped by the cadence, a hold's drain added. Hard and soft are the same run at 1.3x and 0.85x punch power. For The Maw at Full Resonance the row is a Maw that breaks, and "holds" is one that does not.
- **vs Std** is the length against the same mission at Standard (a Maw that holds, for the boss row). Assist and Operator should sit within 5%, Full Resonance at +10% give or take 5%.
- **Card** is the range the card shows at that tier; **Formula** is `WorldMap.expected_minutes` (hard puncher's clean run to an average one with half again for misses). **On card** means the estimate, rounded to a whole minute, is inside the card.
- **Pressure/min** is phantoms answered per minute times speed times 0.88 s over the tell: more, faster, shorter-telling phantoms are more pressure. Contact damage is not in it.
